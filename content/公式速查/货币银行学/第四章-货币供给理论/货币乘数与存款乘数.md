## 问题

基础货币、货币乘数（狭义与广义）和存款乘数（活期、定期、总存款）的公式分别是什么？

## 答案

**7. 基础货币（B）**

$$
M_S = B \times m \implies B = \frac{M_S}{m}
$$

---

**8. 货币乘数**

货币乘数 = 货币供应量 / 基础货币

**(1) 狭义货币乘数（$m_1$）**

$$
m_1 = \frac{M_1}{B} = \frac{C+D}{C+R} = \frac{C+D}{C+RR+ER+TR}
$$

$$
= \frac{\dfrac{C}{D}+1}{\dfrac{C}{D}+\dfrac{RR}{D}+\dfrac{ER}{D}+\dfrac{TR}{T}\times\dfrac{T}{D}} = \frac{1+c}{c+r_d+e+tr}
$$

**(2) 广义货币乘数（$m_2$）**

$$
m_2 = \frac{M_2}{B} = \frac{C+D+T}{C+R} = \frac{C+D+T}{C+RR+ER+TR}
$$

$$
= \frac{1+\dfrac{C}{D}+\dfrac{T}{D}}{\dfrac{C}{D}+\dfrac{RR}{D}+\dfrac{ER}{D}+\dfrac{TR}{T}\times\dfrac{T}{D}} = \frac{1+c+t}{c+r_d+e+tr_t}
$$

---

**9. 存款乘数**

**(1) 活期存款乘数**

$$
\frac{D}{B} = \frac{D}{C+R} = \frac{D}{C+RR+ER+TR}
$$

$$
= \frac{1}{\dfrac{C}{D}+\dfrac{RR}{D}+\dfrac{ER}{D}+\dfrac{TR}{T}\times\dfrac{T}{D}} = \frac{1}{c+r_d+e+tr_t}
$$

**(2) 定期存款乘数**

定期存款乘数 = T（定期存款）/ B（原始存款）

$$
= \frac{T}{D} \times \frac{D}{B} = \frac{t}{c+r_d+e+tr}
$$

**(3) 总存款乘数**

总存款乘数 = (D+T)（活期存款 + 定期存款）/ B（原始存款）

$$
= \frac{D}{B} + \frac{T}{B} = \frac{1+t}{c+r_d+e+tr_t}
$$
