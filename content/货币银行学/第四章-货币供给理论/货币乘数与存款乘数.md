## 问题

基础货币、货币乘数（m₁、m₂）与存款乘数的公式？

## 答案

### 7. 基础货币（B）

$$M_S = B \times m \Rightarrow B = \frac{M_S}{m}$$

### 8. 货币乘数

货币乘数 = 货币供应量 / 基础货币

**(1) 狭义货币乘数（m₁）**

$$m_1 = \frac{M_1}{B} = \frac{C+D}{C+R} = \frac{C+D}{C+RR+ER+TR} = \frac{C/D+1}{C/D+RR/D+ER/D+TR/D} = \frac{1+c}{c+r_d+e+tr}$$

**(2) 广义货币乘数（m₂）**

$$m_2 = \frac{M_2}{B} = \frac{C+D+T}{C+R} = \frac{C+D+T}{C+RR+ER+TR} = \frac{C/D+1}{C/D+RR/D+ER/D+TR/D} \times \frac{T}{D} = \frac{1+c+t}{c+r_d+e+tr_t}$$

### 9. 存款乘数

**(1) 活期存款乘数**

$$\text{活期存款乘数} = \frac{D}{B} = \frac{C+R}{1} = \frac{D}{C+RR+ER+TR} = \frac{1}{c+r_d+e+tr_t}$$

**(2) 定期存款乘数**

$$\text{定期存款乘数} = T \cdot (D/B) = \frac{T}{D} \times \frac{D}{B} = \frac{t}{c+r_d+e+tr_t}$$

（定期存款乘数）= T（定期存款）/B（原始存款）

$$= (D/B) \times (T/D) = \frac{c + r_d + e + tr_t}{1+t} \times \frac{T}{D}$$

**(3) 总存款乘数**

总存款乘数 = (D+T)（活期存款 + 定期存款）/B（原始存款）

$$= (D/B) + (T/B) = \frac{1+t}{c+r_d+e+tr_t}$$
